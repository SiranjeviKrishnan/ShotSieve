### 1. "The picture looks worse than the actual image" (diagnosis, measured)
Method (`scripts/viewer_quality_report.py`, reproducible, originals read-only): the real loupe in Chromium at Fit on **3440x1440 @1x** (the ultrawide) and **1728x1117 @2x** (a Retina panel), screenshot of the picture, compared with (b) the camera's embedded JPEG / the original JPEG converted to sRGB by its ICC profile and Lanczos-resized to the displayed size, and (c) macOS `sips` (Apple's decoder, sRGB-matched) resized the same way = ground truth. Causes found (before -> after, 3440x1440@1x / 1728x1117@2x):
1. **Upscaled, blurry (the main one).** The loupe always got a 1600 px preview, q88, made as a 1/2..1/8 DCT decode + INTER_AREA + (in the browser) a second resize.
