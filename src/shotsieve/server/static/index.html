    Suggestions [ ]

    Select several photos

    ClickSelect one photo
    Cmd+clickAdd a photo to the selection or remove it
    Shift+clickSelect the range from the last clicked photo (in the grid's current order)
    Shift+← → ↑ ↓Extend the selection
    Cmd+A / EscSelect every visible photo / clear the selection
    Ctrl+clickOn a Mac this is a right-click: it opens the menu (for the whole selection when you click inside it)
    P X 0–9Keep, reject, stars and colour labels apply to every selected photo
    Cmd+DeleteMove the selected photos to the Trash (asks first; recoverable with Undo or Put Back in Finder; Cmd+Z undoes)

    Filters

    Cmd+FSearch (same as /)
    Shift+FShow / hide the filter bar (filters stay active while it is hidden)
    Filter barPeople, Picked / Unflagged / Rejected, colour labels, stars 0-5, and More: file type, orientation, warnings, faces, near-duplicates, camera and lens. Choices inside one group are OR, different groups are AND; they are remembered per shoot
    Key facesSide panel: click a face to filter by that person

    Faces and Lightroom

    Shift+PPeople: every recognised person (rename, merge, ignore, show their photos)
    FacesNames and fixes live in the people strip, the Faces row of the side panel, People and Key faces (the loupe no longer draws face boxes)
    Cmd+LOpen the selected image in Lightroom (RAW master; the XMP sidecar carries the rating and crop)

    Near-identical frames

    CCompare the group's best with the current image (click a file name in "Why" to open that frame)
    1 2 / ← →In compare: choose the frame to keep
    EnterIn compare: keep the chosen frame, reject the other
    ↑ ↓In compare: next frame of the group
    LIn compare: link / unlink the zoom and pan of the two sides

    Zoom (loupe and compare)

    DDenoise for viewing (main preview, loupe and compare; in compare Shift+D only the side under the pointer). Strength Low / Medium / High in the arrow next to the button; your files are never changed
    Z / double-clickToggle Fit and 100 % (actual pixels) at the cursor
    + − 0Zoom in / out / back to Fit (also Cmd+= Cmd+− Cmd+0)
    Pinch / Ctrl-wheelZoom around the cursor
    Two-finger scroll / dragPan while zoomed (the corner thumbnail can be dragged too)
    ← →Each photo remembers its own zoom and position; a photo you never zoomed opens at Fit
    Crop modeZoom is off while cropping (leaving crop returns to Fit)

    Subject mode (S, in the loupe)

    SSubject mode: the cursor becomes a crosshair; drag to draw a box around your subject (up to 6). Corners resize, drag inside to move, X / Delete removes the selected box
    ScoreEach box shows its sharpness (the same 0-1 scale as a face's sharpness): "in focus" or "soft", measured on the full-resolution pixels
    Esc / SLeave subject mode (the boxes stay, and crop mode keeps them in the crop)

    Crop mode (R)

    RCrop mode: AI crop suggestions (Enter accepts, Backspace rejects, X flips, [ ] cycle, Esc leaves)
    ClickA subject: keep it inside the crop (Shift adds more)
    DragDraw a box around your own subject (corners resize, Delete removes it)
    EscClear the selection, then leave
